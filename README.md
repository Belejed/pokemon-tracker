## 🌟 Overview

**Pokémon Tracker** is a client-side web application crafted for Pokémon TCG collectors, hobbyists, and store owners to track their collection asset value, log buy/sell transactions, and visualize monthly cash flows in real-time without requiring complex backend setups.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📊 **Main Cash Ledger & Portfolio Valuation**:
  - Real-time calculation of **Cash Balance**, **Sales Income**, **Expense (Procurement)**, and **Stored Asset Net Worth**.
- 📈 **Visual Financial Analytics**:
  - Interactive monthly cash flow bar charts and asset category breakdown pie charts powered by **Chart.js**.
- 🎴 **Inventory & Card Catalog**:
  - Full-featured card showcase with instant search filtering, rarity tags, quantity trackers, and market price valuation.
- 💸 **Transaction History Log**:
  - Complete mutasi bookkeeping recording buy/sell transactions with date, category, and nominal values.
- 🎯 **Wishlist & Hunt List**:
  - Dedicated tracker for upcoming card targets with estimated pricing.
- 🔐 **Role-Based Access Control**:
  - **Admin Mode**: Add, edit, delete cards, and record transactions.
  - **Guest / Read-Only Mode**: Public showcase mode allowing visitors to view collection stats safely.
- 📥 **Accounting Data Export**:
  - Instant export to **CSV / Excel** for external bookkeeping and auditing.
- 📱 **Mobile-First Responsive Design**:
  - Floating desktop navigation and dedicated bottom app bar for seamless smartphone viewing.
